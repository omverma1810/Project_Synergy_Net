08 — Returns

Investment Returns

Investors in the LLC receive 100% of Adjusted Gross Proceeds until they have recouped {{ returns.recoup_multiple }} of their investment. Thereafter, investors receive their proportionate share of {{ returns.profit_split_investor }} of net profit in perpetuity; the remaining {{ returns.profit_split_other }} is allocated to cast & crew profit participation.

Risks. A substantial number of films do not achieve profitability. Returns depend on unpredictable factors including critical reception, timing, and public taste, and investors in an unsuccessful film may not recoup their investment. All investors should conduct their own due diligence and consult their own legal, accounting, and tax advisors.
